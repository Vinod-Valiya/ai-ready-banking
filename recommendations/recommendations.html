The white papers in this series are grounded in real delivery. The two letters below, from a major non-bank lender and a member-owned bank, describe the kinds of core banking, payments, and batch-optimization work the series writes about.

Australian Military Bank · Appreciation

Rizwan Dean, Head of Information Technology Operations & Security - 9 July 2026

Rizwan Dean wrote in his personal capacity as Head of IT Operations & Security to commend work on the bank's Finacle environment, delivered as part of the Infosys team. The letter points to concrete, measured improvements rather than general praise.

What the letter documents

  • End of Day (EOD) completion time cut from seven hours daily to one hour, lifting the availability of critical banking systems.
  • Time-based payment jobs reduced from 30 minutes to 90 minutes, down to 5 to 10 minutes.
  • Purging of historical payment data, streamlining transaction handling and payment operation.
  • Resolution of NPP payment timing-out issues through improved payment transaction handling.
  • Reduction of PCTR unmatched card transactions by resolving card message timeouts.
  • Improvements to the hybrid payroll upload process for faster, more reliable uploads.

The letter closes by noting that EOD and time-based payment jobs now routinely complete within an hour, with improved performance and reliability across both CORE and Channel applications for staff and members.

Resimac · Recommendation

Paul Groom, Senior Product Owner - To whom it may concern

Paul Groom recommended Vinod in recognition of leadership, innovation, and dedication in delivering the enterprise-scale modernization of Resimac's Core Banking and Cards platforms. The engagement supported more than 40 million transactions over four years.

What the letter documents

  • Modernization of Resimac's Core Banking and Cards platforms, establishing a foundation for future growth.
  • Delivery of next-generation digital banking capabilities improving customer experience and operational efficiency.
  • Real-time transaction processing architecture, improving transaction speed, availability, and responsiveness.
  • A 60% reduction in batch processing windows, accelerating end-of-day processing and increasing agility.
  • A scalable, secure, and future-ready banking ecosystem able to support evolving business requirements.

The letter recommends Vinod without hesitation for senior leadership roles involving large-scale technology transformation, digital banking modernization, and enterprise platform delivery.
